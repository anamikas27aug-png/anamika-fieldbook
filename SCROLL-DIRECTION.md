## Grammar and gate
Interactive fieldbook: cover, working pages, project specimens, connected background and a collected-note ending. Bans: continuous worldflight, fake product screenshots, blanket mobile motion cutoffs, repeated headline entrances, a fading final CTA, hover-dependent meaning.
Filmic one-shot prevents jumping; editorial title-page grammar bans the portrait-led cover; live surface misrepresents a portfolio as a shipped tool; worldflight lacks meaningful geography; typographic poster removes needed project specimens; gallery alone omits reasoning and career; split stage imposes a false binary; cutlist undermines the notebook's sustained change.
Gate against rejected v1: at least 5/6 dimensions differ. Gate against curiosity studio: 5/6 differ (grammar, bookmark nav, act sequence, collected-card ending, saved-evidence signature); photographic portrait depth is deliberately shared. Keep both historical rows.
